NumTrace /num-treys/

noun

1. The act of tracing a phone number to its country, network, and approximate region — without tracking the owner.
2. An ethical tool that reveals what a number actually contains, and asks for consent before showing live location.
Example: "I used NumTrace to confirm the number was MTN Nigeria before I called back."
